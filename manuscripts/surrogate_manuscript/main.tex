\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
High-fidelity large-eddy simulation (LES) of urban airflow is prohibitively
expensive for real-time data assimilation. We present a neural surrogate based
on the \pthreed{} hybrid CNN-Transformer architecture \cite{holzschuh2025p3d} that
replaces the \palm{} LES solver \cite{maronga2020} inside an Ensemble Smoother
with Multiple Data Assimilation (\esmda{}) loop \cite{emerick2013}. The surrogate
operates on the three-component velocity field over a $224\times224\times32$
Barcelona urban-block grid and takes inflow parameters as explicit conditioning
inputs, enabling joint state-parameter estimation. A pushforward rollout
curriculum stabilises multi-step prediction, and a training-data warm-start
strategy avoids CFD spin-up cost at inference time. On a held-out \palm{} truth
trajectory the surrogate-driven \esmda{} recovers the inflow direction (angle RMSE
reduced by $34\%$ relative to the prior) and a spatially faithful posterior
velocity field in roughly $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$ of single-GPU wall time, with the
street-level velocity amplitude limited by sensor observability.
\end{abstract}
