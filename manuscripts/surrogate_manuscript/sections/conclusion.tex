\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------

We have presented a \pthreed{}-based neural surrogate for urban airflow that enables
fast ensemble data assimilation over a realistic Barcelona urban-block geometry.
The surrogate conditions on explicit inflow parameters, enforces building geometry
via a hard mask, and is trained with a pushforward rollout curriculum that
promotes multi-step stability. Embedded in an \esmda{} framework, it supports
inflow-parameter and state estimation from six street-level point sensors, with
ensemble initialisation from \palm{} training snapshots eliminating spin-up cost. The
training-data warm start and the \palm{}-vs-surrogate truth/assimilation model
pairing together provide a principled and practically efficient evaluation
framework.

On a held-out \palm{} truth trajectory (Section~\ref{sec:da_results}), the
surrogate-driven \esmda{} recovers the inflow direction---reducing the angle RMSE
by $34\%$ relative to the prior---and produces a spatially faithful posterior
velocity field whose error stays bounded across all four windows, all in
$\approx 1.5\,\mathrm{h}$ of single-GPU wall time. The main limitation is the
inflow velocity magnitude, whose street-level signal is only weakly observable
from six point sensors; denser or better-placed sensing, rather than a larger
ensemble or a different update scheme, is the natural next step.
